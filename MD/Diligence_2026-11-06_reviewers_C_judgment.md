## Financial audit disposition
The independently specified **M002 reconstruction** of the M001/M004 boundaries is acceptable as an auditable illustration, conditional on visible assumptions and unforced differences from supplied targets. It is **not** a reproduction of an undisclosed original model, a forecast, or a valuation opinion.

The root model reviewed uses annual2027–2036 end-year cash flows,15% real discount, zero terminal business value, center×ears-per-center capacity,1.15ears/patient, defined cost learning, capital and development schedules, working capital and simplified21% tax/NOL treatment. The original supplied NPV/peak-funding/price thresholds must remain separate targets. Price×volume and launch-delay sensitivities are conditional recomputations, not probabilities.

At the reviewed model version, base M001 NPV15 was about-$68.7M before runoff and-$72.5M after modeled support runoff; best was+$99.2M and+$87.1M respectively. M004 base was about-$0.97M before runoff and-$4.65M after; upside+$10.24M and+$4.72M. Numerical results can move if the root updates assumptions; final tables must be generated from the delivered model, not copied from this audit note.

**Material qualifications accepted into synthesis:**
1. Best2036 capacity27,550ears is approximately48.6% of Cochlear's FY26 worldwide56,692unit scale. Different years are not market-share denominators. This is a demanding distribution assumption requiring named-center commitments, trained surgeons, theatre capacity, referrals and payer approvals.
2. Year10 manufacturing overhead is calibrated to reproduce the supplied COP/COM relation; that calibration must be disclosed. A matched COM does not validate the underlying factory cost estimate.
3. Support runoff after2036 has been added as a sensitivity. It is a **minimal variable-cost/no-future-revenue runoff calculation**, not a validated full actuarial liability. Fixed safety support, product recall, replacement, explant and indemnity exposures remain separately unresolved.
4. The software runoff cost with no later subscription revenue is a downside treatment; whether it is legally owed depends on contracts. Do not label every modeled retained user's fee-free support as an established liability.
5. Tax21% with full modeled NOL offset omits jurisdiction detail and applicable NOL restrictions; cash/FCI is not conventional total-capital ROI. The revised model names cash/FCI and cash return over development plus FCI separately. IRS2025Form1120 instructions independently confirm post2017NOL80% constraints; no complete tax opinion was attempted.
6. Delay scenarios must explain which development/capex/fixed overhead continue, which launch-dependent costs shift, and that the forecast still ends2036. A delay removes late sales from this fixed horizon.
7. No terminal value means no value assigned to later enterprise cash flows, not that future duties vanish. No working-capital release is included in the reviewed convention.
8. Seven-year support and one-to-two-year calibration are modeling horizons, not validated survival, clinical utility or approval timelines.
9. $1,200 or$1,800 annual fee,60% or90% attach,88% retention and$300 support floor need paid-pilot/contract evidence. Break-even fee from the reconstructed model must not replace the supplied ~$1,461 target without the variance explanation.

## Release judgment
The $1.5M first-tranche ceiling is an illustrative allocation for rights, signal falsification, anatomy/materials, isolation/security and actual demand investigation. It is not sufficient evidence of full program funding. Later tranches release on data and independent benefit-risk review, not elapsed calendar time. No2030 or2032 marketing date is authorized by this analysis.

**Most consequential empirical limitation:** safe chronic acquisition of stable, speech-specific information from the proposed pharyngeal site, with meaningful benefit beyond non-implant alternatives. Neither underwriting nor model agreement can close it.
