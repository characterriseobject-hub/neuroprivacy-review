## Decision
**M001: Hold whole-program investment; Conditional-Go for bounded Layer A and external Layer B diligence. Layer C: No-Go for an elective chronic tonsillar decoding/stimulation product commitment now; Conditional-Go only for nonclinical falsification work. M004: Conditional-Go as a separately priced software option only after documentary confirmation of the counterfactual sponsor approval, rights, interfaces, liability and support obligations.**

Neither two years of calibration nor an MIT relationship supplies FDA authorization, clinical utility, reimbursement or market demand. Conversely, a lack of tonsillar evidence does not invalidate conventional cochlear implantation or a genuinely external patient-controlled communication app. These are separate products with different predicates and clinical questions.

## Stage 1: competing candidate models
1. **Indication-led staged build (preferred):** preserve the hearing indication and fault-isolated core; develop optional communication software with no implant-control or diagnostic claims first; make the pharyngeal extension a separate research hypothesis and capital gate. This minimizes cross-contamination of the hearing program's benefit-risk case.
2. **Integrated sponsor platform (best alternative):** a manufacturer with a current PMA and clinical infrastructure sponsors the combination. A supplement may be feasible, but access to its dossier, design control, quality responsibilities and written FDA feedback are prerequisites. Novel anatomical hardware/indications may require a major supplement or new PMA; a partner name does not decide the route.
3. **Software option (counterfactual M004):** assume the sponsor already financed and obtained the specific 2030 approved combination and licenses. Value incremental software cash flows only. That option can be economically coherent even when M001 is unattractive, but it is conditional on an asset not established by public evidence. Its NPV cannot be added to a whole-program NPV that already contains the same software cash flows.

## Consent and mental privacy
Patient preview/confirmation/quarantine before discretionary AI-summary export is a defensible **product requirement**. It must not be described as an absolute existing statutory veto over every clinician record or legally required safety report. HIPAA permits amendment requests and disagreement statements, with enumerated grounds for denial; amendment records are appended or linked [SRM011]. GDPR special-category processing and rectification rights also have specified scope [SRM025]. The architecture must distinguish the patient's discretionary assistant content from mandatory safety-event reporting and clinician-authored care documentation. Preserve the provenance of disagreements without silently replacing the clinical record.

P960009/S478 supports an analogy of bounded clinician programming and a patient-accessible pause: pause returns adaptive DBS to programmed conventional DBS, rather than turning all stimulation off. It does **not** establish a regulatory precedent for semantic privacy, thought transcription, tonsillar implants or a patient-exclusive hardware kill switch [SRM006]. The proposed stronger PATL policy needs its own verified implementation.

NICE NG155 supports stepped tinnitus-distress psychological care after support, with qualified practitioners. It does not establish autonomous CBT or recommend chronic tonsillar neuromodulation [SRM010]. Device artifacts must be considered, but screening should not require clinicians to prove the absence of every otological cause before responding to patient-reported distress. Coexisting neurological, otological and mental-health needs remain possible. The software can block its own unvalidated inference without obstructing requested human assessment.

A consumer waiver cannot repeal device law; research consent cannot contain exculpatory language that waives apparent legal rights or releases sponsor/investigator negligence liability under21CFR50.20 [SRM023]. HCP-in-loop is a control, not a classification exemption.

## Clinical validation design (proposals, not approved protocols)
**Layer A:** compare hearing performance, implant safety and reliability with an accepted hearing-device benchmark in the intended population. Prespecify sentence and word measures, speech in noise, patient-reported listening effort, battery/charge burden, surgical complications, device failure and explant/revision. Measure performance when elective modules are disabled. Do not use a neural-decoder endpoint as a substitute for hearing benefit.

**Layer B:** randomized crossover of core hearing support alone versus core plus CoPilot, using comparable training and washout where appropriate. Evaluate speech in noise, communication effort, error burden, daily task completion and user preference. Include existing manufacturer support tools and suitable consumer assistive alternatives in a pragmatic comparison. WTP must use real price tradeoffs/deposit or paid pilot evidence rather than only satisfaction. Keep medical therapeutic claims outside the exploratory consumer test unless the protocol/authorization supports them.

**Layer C:** first determine whether pharyngeal recordings contain information beyond swallowing, movement, acoustic leakage and task timing. Use blinded labels, held-out days and participants, unseen phrases, contemporaneous microphone/EMG nuisance channels, silent/no-intent conditions and explicit abstention. Establish whether successful output depends on residual articulation; this is not interchangeable with inner-speech decoding. Before chronic human implantation, require a justified anatomical placement method, chronic infection/erosion/explant evidence, electrical/thermal safety, biocompatibility and hardware reliability. Neuromodulation requires a separate indication, dose/safety rationale and clinical benefit design. Final sample size cannot be responsibly asserted without a prespecified endpoint, clinically meaningful margin, variance, expected attrition and regulatory consultation.

The **single most consequential unresolved empirical limitation** is whether a chronically safe tonsillar/submucosal interface can yield stable, speech-specific information with meaningful incremental communication utility. More compute, a long calibration horizon and an approved cochlear component do not answer that question.

**Economic candidate conclusion:** supplied M001/M004 NPV figures are scenario targets, not published issuer valuations. Annual regional volumes, clinical spend, operating expenses, working capital, tax, capex timing, support tail and attribution rules are required to reproduce them. An independent transparent reconstruction is preferable to quietly fitting cash flows to attractive targets, provided it remains clearly labeled. US launch2032/base and2030/best are assumptions, not regulator schedules. Y2 commercial revenue must remain zero.

**Required model challenge:** a seven-year support promise creates liabilities beyond2036 for late cohorts. Zero terminal business value does not cancel these costs. Tax21% with full NOL utilization is an illustrative simplification, not a jurisdiction-complete tax model. A cash/FCI ratio should not be labeled conventional ROI on total invested capital. For M004, retain sponsor-funded hardware/clinical/safety responsibilities in an excluded-cost ledger; low incremental software capex does not establish low total program cost. A90% attach and$1,800fee upside needs independent demand evidence. Center ramps need training capacity, operating-room time, referral conversion and payer authorization evidence; multiplying active centers by unconstrained throughput is not a market forecast.

## Evidence limitations
Regulatory sources are current public material, not an agency determination for this design. Full dossiers, adverse-event raw data, insurer contracts, named-center commitments, sponsor budgets and executed MIT licenses were unavailable. The EU MDR source used includes January2026 consolidation plus April2026 classification guidance; latest amendment reconciliation remains open. The software option remains counterfactual, and a proprietary source financial workbook was not supplied. Source completeness does not imply complete due diligence.
