## Retained empirical conclusion
Published cortical speech interfaces demonstrate substantial capability in their studied participants. The modalities, tasks, denominators and overlapping cohorts remain distinct. In particular, Metzger used cortical-surface ECoG; Card/Wairagkar reuse T15; Kunz's cortical inner-speech results and probabilistic lock do not establish either tonsillar information content or deterministic mental-privacy protection.

For UmboMic, the reviewed evidence supports prototype acoustic transduction in cadaveric human temporal bones and fixation/function work in cadaveric sheep. Comens is not chronic living-animal evidence. Reported modeled outer-ear sensitivity must remain distinguished from measured sensor output. None of this establishes the proposed combined chronic human product.

The decisive unresolved empirical question is **whether a chronically safe pharyngeal/tonsillar interface produces stable, intent-specific information that improves communication beyond external sensors under prospective nuisance-controlled testing**. Signal information and chronic tissue safety are jointly necessary; long calibration cannot substitute for either.

## Audit findings accepted
1. **C's non-authorizing gate correction is mandatory.** PASS in impedance, SNR, artifact and calibration checks only permits otherwise validated processing. It never authorizes psychiatric diagnosis or rules out otological/neurological disease. Patient self-report and requested human care remain accessible.
2. **B's material/anatomical distinctions accepted.** Flexible thin film is not necessarily hermetic; implanted muscle recording is implanted EMG; ENG requires selectivity evidence; cuffs and meshes are not interchangeable.
3. **A's timing correction accepted.** The illustrative 3.2-kbps feature stream at 10 Hz has a 100-ms cadence. Keep it separate from the ≤5-ms local hearing target.
4. **Rights are requirements, not observed achievements.** PATL OFF must stop local elective activity and stale output deterministically. Previously transmitted data cannot be recalled by a switch; cloud cancellation/deletion needs separate contracts and verification.
5. **Metzger correction retained.** Channel-order errors affected anatomical assignments/maps; reported real-time performance was unaffected. Use updated maps without dismissing valid outcome data.
6. **Commercial optimism challenged symmetrically.** A narrow articulatory command system remains scientifically plausible and testable, but is not yet evidence of inner-speech decoding, seven-year survival, $1,200 willingness-to-pay or regulatory authorization.
7. **Misattribution hazard bounded.** General confabulation and automation bias are supported concerns; no verified incidence supports the prompt's “frequently” for the proposed integrated device. Avoid both automated psychiatric labeling and the opposite assertion that all symptoms are device artifacts.

## Release-changing experiments
A decisive favorable result requires prospective held-out-day/participant performance above nuisance-only, language-model-only and external-sensor baselines; acceptable false outputs during no-intent conditions; demonstrable calibration stability; and an independently acceptable chronic safety plan. A decisive negative result is loss of decoding advantage after acoustic/movement/task-cue controls, unacceptable chronic tissue injury, or inability to preserve essential hearing through elective-domain faults. Thresholds must be preregistered for the intended clinical task before data inspection.

No claim of complete publication-grade scientific verification is made for inaccessible methods or unreviewed full dossiers. A_sources.json and the peer ledgers state actual access scope and outstanding evidence.
