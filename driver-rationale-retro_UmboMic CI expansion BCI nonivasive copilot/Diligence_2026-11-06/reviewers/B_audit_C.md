# Stage 2 — Reviewer B audits Reviewer C

Date/cutoff: 2026-11-06. Candidate read: C.md and C_sources.json. This is a challenge review, not legal certification or an independent empirical replication.

| Vulnerability | Audit evidence / challenge | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| A broad “every implant/software requires PMA” narrative could conceal real function-specific routes | C distinguishes implant PMA, possible supplements, device/nondevice app functions and De Novo criteria | **Accept.** Retain individualized intended-use and risk analysis; neither HCP oversight nor waiver bypasses it |
| Favorable M004 boundary may shift costs on paper without enforceable sponsor responsibility | C explicitly requires the *combined* approved platform, interfaces, rights, liability and support responsibilities | **Accept with contractual gate.** Add recalls, cybersecurity patches, explant/revision, postapproval changes and insolvency continuity to the excluded-cost schedule |
| CNS approval analogy could be misused for tonsillar safety/privacy | C correctly limits Percept adaptive-DBS analogy and notes pause returns to conventional programmed stimulation | **Accept.** No safety or semantic privacy inheritance |
| Anti-overshadowing rule might itself delay necessary care | C distinguishes software artifact suppression from blocking patient-requested human assessment | **Accept.** No autonomous psychiatric labels, and no assertion that psychiatric and otological needs are mutually exclusive |
| Medicare threshold could be mistaken for blanket access or software reimbursement | B reopened official NCD50.3 and verified its additional candidacy, infection/anatomy and FDA-label conditions | **Accept.** NCD is not a volume commitment, payment rate or tonsillar/SaaS coverage |
| Regulatory currency: cybersecurity guidance or AI Act timeline could be stale | B reopened FDA119933 cover: February3,2026 supersedes June27,2025. EUR-Lex direct open challenged, but indexed consolidatedArticle113 and current Commission/Council pages corroborate December2,2027 AnnexIII and August2,2028 AnnexI timing | **Accept C dates within retrieved scope.** Record authority/version; current consolidated text is documentation and amended acts control |
| Two-year clinical/calibration promise might hide chronic evidence gap | C requires nonclinical safety, controlled signal-identifiability evidence and authorized study gates | **Accept.** Calendar time alone cannot release a tranche; seven-year support is not seven-year survival evidence |
| Trial comparator could give the platform an artificially weak opponent | C requires optimized core hearing, existing support tools and suitable consumer alternatives | **Accept with endpoint discipline.** Name device configuration, language, training and participant residual articulation; compare implanted incremental utility against external EMG before accepting added surgical risk |
| ROI and zero-terminal-value modeling could omit late liabilities | C identified support tail and cash/FCI naming issue; root now includes a tail reserve sensitivity and calls ratio cash/FCI | **Accept correction as a modeled reserve.** It is not an actuarially validated full recall/explant liability |
| Positive best-case arithmetic might imply attainable distribution | Root model inspected:2036 best conditional units=27,550 (15,000US+8,750secondary+3,800Asia), versus CochlearFY26 benchmark56,692 worldwide ears | **Challenge retained.** About49% of incumbent annual unit scale in targeted markets is an aggressive conditional assumption, not a validated bottom-up funnel merely because expressed as centers×throughput |
| Supplied NPV could appear reproduced after arbitrary cost allocation | C calls supplied figures scenario targets and requires disclosed reconstruction | **Accept.** Present supplied targets and calculated outputs side by side; explain timing, centers, overhead, tax and support-tail differences without forcing equality |
| Low outsourced inference cost could imply low safety-support cost | C's sponsor and lifecycle boundary is explicit | **Accept with stress test.** Clinical triage, accessibility support, cybersecurity incidents and multilingual validation can dominate token expense; attach/retention and support-hours need pilot data |

Financial reconstruction is useful when its assumptions and unmatched supplied targets remain visible. The combined safe-and-useful pharyngeal signal remains the decisive empirical gap.
