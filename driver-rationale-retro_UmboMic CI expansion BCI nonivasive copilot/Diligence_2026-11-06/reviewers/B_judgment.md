## Decision record

1. **Layer A Conditional-Go for engineering diligence**, subject to chronic packaging/fixation, energy reliability and intended-population hearing evidence. “Validated hearing core” means established cochlear restoration as a mechanism; this specific UmboMic integration remains investigational. Its studied evidence is human cadaver and cadaveric sheep, with no chronic clinical credit imported from MED-EL/Envoy.
2. **Layer B Conditional-Go for external prototype and comparative testing.** All audio into the core is authenticated, bounded and subordinate to its local safety controller. Price/attach are demand hypotheses. Auracast is an external broadcast selection path, not an implant-neural uplink.
3. **Rigid tonsillar Utah configuration No-Go for elective chronic human use in the present evidence state.** Serious prospective mechanical/infection/vascular hazards and absent useful-signal evidence make a product commitment unjustified. This is not an observed complication-rate claim or a universal impossibility theorem.
4. **Conformable submucosal interface Hold; nonclinical falsification Conditional-Go.** Preserve implanted EMG versus surface EMG versus selective ENG distinction. Compliance does not establish hermeticity; a cuff and broad mesh are different interventions. Require performance against nuisance controls and external sensors before accepting surgical risk.
5. **M004 cannot override physics.** Even under assumed sponsor approval, available signal, supported API and approved intended use constrain software. A change enabling inner-speech or therapeutic behavior outside that scope may reopen validation and regulatory work. Hardware, recall, explant, aftercare and cyber responsibilities remain with a named funded party.

## Adopted corrections after A's audit

- Keep **≤5ms hearing-loop maximum as a proposed validation target** distinct from the **100ms cadence** of the illustrative10Hz feature compressor. Neither channel count nor3.2kbps establishes linguistic information or end-to-end decoder latency. Perform rate–distortion/task testing.
- Shared chargers, buses, clocks and firmware updating are common-cause failure paths. Demonstrate that auxiliary brownout, queue corruption, relay compromise and off-switch transition cannot disable essential hearing or exceed stimulation bounds.
- Patient OFF must prevent new elective capture/processing/egress locally without remote reactivation. Previously transmitted data cannot be instantly “untransmitted”; provider retention/deletion and credential invalidation require separate controls.
- Psychiatric-proxy outputs and hidden labeling from candidate code are rejected by design; physiological inputs may inform only appropriately validated signal quality/processing. Patient reports remain reports; a failed artifact check routes to device review/abstention, without suppressing requested clinical assessment.
- US **M-EIRP**, EU **ERP**, occupied bandwidth, gross PHY rate and useful payload must each retain units and definitions. Ordinary voice cannot be assumed permissible on MedRadio. The illustrative RF budget remains a sensitivity calculation pending measurements.
- Treat15–22mm carotid separation as an unverified generalized planning number, never a clearance guarantee. Pediatric primary evidence includes much smaller distances and cannot be extrapolated into a particular adult's surgical geometry.

## Architecture acceptance evidence required

| Gate | Evidence to accept | Failure disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Essential hearing separation | Local hearing performance/latency and stimulation bounds under enumerated single faults and selected common-cause faults | Hold any integrated clinical release |
| PATL and privacy | Physical-state tests, broker enforcement, blocked cloud calls, queue flush, disabled context persistence, no remote restart | No-Go to elective data collection until repaired |
| Signal utility | Locked, preregistered held-out task validation; no-intent/swallow/cough/sound controls; external comparator | Stop invasive decoder branch if no justified incremental utility |
| Chronic tissue interface | Appropriate mechanical, packaging, biological and chronic histology evidence; safe removal concept | Hold human study; redesign or stop |
| Telemetry | Measured representative-body goodput, power, coexistence and worst-case margins with legal waveform review | Reduce payload or redesign route; no assumed BLE guarantee |
| Support lifecycle | Funded7year support and rescue obligations, update process, replacement and withdrawal/explant plan | No commercial commitment |

These gates are proposals to make the diligence decision testable. They are not already passed, not an approved study protocol and not a declaration of regulatory compliance. **Final architecture verdict: staged Conditional-Go for A/B research; Hold for flexible LayerC; No-Go for proposed rigid mucosal penetrating array.**
