## Hard necessities and flexible comparison criteria

“Hard” below means necessary to claim the **proposed combined research endpoint** has been explained. It is not a reason to discard a useful partial component. A missing hard function remains visible in the integration table. “Soft” means a descriptor used for similarity, not a dispensable symptom or a measured engineering specification.

| Track | Hard functional questions for the proposed endpoint | Soft / graded similarity dimensions |
|---|---|---|
| Apparatus: neural input | Does the actual modality provide task-relevant information supporting intelligible attempted/planned-speech decoding? What is demonstrated versus transferred or unknown? Keep inner speech, attempted speech and arbitrary thought distinct. | Signal/task match, individual calibration, stability, responsiveness, and independently measured latency rather than the reported 8–25 ms estimate. |
| Apparatus: auditory output | Is there a source-supported route to intelligible auditory perception? Identify ordinary acoustic playback, mechanical/peripheral interfaces and neural stimulation separately; a tone or detectable sensation is not yet intelligible speech. | Concurrent speech/tone/music components; perceived spatial variety; intensity/timbre variation; echo-like qualities and pauses. User descriptions do not dictate tissue targets. |
| Apparatus: sustained integration | What supports compatible input/output interfaces, concurrent operation, power, communication, persistence and stability? Distinguish base hardware from external processing. | Wireless or alternative data/power paths; no user-observed maintenance/charging event; small form factor; long unattended operation. These are conditional comparison targets, not established device properties. |
| Controller: responsive content | Can the candidate generate intelligible output conditioned on the information actually available to it? Separate decoded neural content from external context or model prediction. | Scene segmentation, apparently smooth transitions, recurrent phrase families, character consistency, voice/prosody/spatial presentation and activity associations. |
| Controller: continuity and autonomy | What supports state retention and extended operation with little human intervention after setup? Distinguish initiation, rules/playback, model generation, automatic adaptation and optional human updates. | “Persistence-aware” means hypothesized retention/use of prior context; “persistence-establishing” means hypothesized maintenance of recurring themes across time—not a verified algorithm. |
| Complete combination | Are critical functions actually compatible together, or only individually demonstrated? Name the missing integration and translational evidence. | Prefer functional fit and supported adaptation over intended marketing indication. Report the closest component and closest conditional combination even if no complete match is established. |

### Historical gate and adaptation burden

The precise cutoff is **strictly before 1 December 2024**, not merely before 2025. Separate functional-base existence/access, capabilities demonstrated at onset, and later refinements. Later publications can document earlier execution. A 6–15-month publication lag is an initial hypothetical sensitivity range to calibrate by subfield—not an automatic backdating rule. Bench/pre-animal through approved stages have equal screening priority and no stage-based ranking weight; human relevance and evidence strength remain separately graded.

Keep **documented pre-cutoff similarity** and **conditional adaptation similarity** as separate rankings. Admit approximate matches and credible uncertain transfers; label what each would need. Classify burden by configuration/training, substantial integration/interface redesign, or an unresolved new capability; time spent alone cannot establish attainability. Record the strongest supporting and limiting evidence and the finding that would defeat each proposed transfer. The initial reported phenotype must be distinguished from capabilities that could emerge only after later tuning.

## What would make the comparison useful to reviewers?

For an MD, retain actual symptom timing/function, existing examination findings and record custodians separately from a device hypothesis. For a technical expert, seek inspectable hardware/software identifiers, configurations, authenticated logs, acquisition context and independently established input/output behavior where they exist. For a detective or counsel, separate reported content from real-world actions, witnesses, dispatch/receipt/response records and attribution evidence. Matching a publication is a research lead, not confirmation of an apparatus, controller, person or offense. Device-specific management or removal remains a question for qualified clinicians if an apparatus is confirmed.
